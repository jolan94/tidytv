Debloat the Xiaomi Mi TV P1 43"

Android TV 11 · 27 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This 43-inch Xiaomi Mi TV P1 is a Latin America set on Android TV 11 with 1.7 GB of memory. It ships with Xiaomi and Google telemetry, Xiaomi content, ads and recommendation rows, background services and preloaded apps. Five more apps are optional.

It was checked after the change: HDMI 1 to 3, AV and Live TV in the Inputs menu, Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video and Max, Chromecast, Miracast and the Bluetooth remote. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Does Netflix still work?

Yes. The Netflix key service is kept, and Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video and Max all launched.

Will Chromecast and Miracast still work?

Yes. Both kept working after the change.

Does it have a way back?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on in one tap, and puts the old home screen back if you changed it.
