Debloat the Xiaomi TV Stick 4K

Android TV 14 · 30 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Xiaomi TV Stick 4K ships with Xiaomi and Google telemetry and attribution, PatchWall content rows, promos and auto-installs. It also has preloaded apps that a stick does not need. Each app has its own switch in the app, so you decide which ones to turn off.

It is a streaming stick with no HDMI-in or tuner. The setup wizard does not claim Home here, so it can stay on and Wi-Fi does not need re-joining. TidyTV saves a backup first and never deletes anything. TidyTV turns these apps off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Can I get the old home screen back on my Xiaomi TV Stick 4K?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap. TidyTV only switches apps off, so nothing is ever deleted.

How do I check for firmware updates?

Switch the Xiaomi update service back on in the app, check for updates, then switch it off again if you like.

Does TidyTV delete anything on my Xiaomi TV Stick 4K?

No. It turns apps off with Android's own setting, saves a backup first and checks each one. You can turn any app back on with its switch.
