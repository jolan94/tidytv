Debloat the Xiaomi Mi Box 3

Android TV 9 · 24 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Xiaomi Mi Box 3 is a streaming box on Android TV 9 with 2 GB of memory. It ships with Xiaomi and Google telemetry and ad tracking, the PatchWall home with content rows, promos and auto-installs, and preloaded apps. Voice, Mi cast and the phone remote are off by choice.

Everything else stayed off after a restart, and the new home screen stayed Home. Home and Back work without any key helper. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything. Each app has its own switch.

What always stays on

  • Chromecast
  • Firmware updates and their download helper
  • The setup helper, which owned a saved Wi-Fi network
  • Xiaomi setup and TV service parts

Good to know on this model

  • Turning off the voice assistant stops the mic button.
  • The Ambient screensaver is turned back on at every start. It is harmless once the screensaver points at the new home screen's clock.
  • It has no HDMI input or tuner.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the mic button still work?

Not if you switch off the voice assistant, which is on the list as a personal choice. Leave it on to keep voice.

Will Chromecast still work?

Yes. Chromecast is kept.

Does it have a way back?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on in one tap, and puts the old home screen back if you changed it.
