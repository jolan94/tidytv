Debloat the Skyworth SWTV-24AE-4K NF

Google TV 14 · 26 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Skyworth set sells in South Africa and runs Google TV on Android 14 with about 1 GB of memory. It ships with Skyworth telemetry and activation, Android ad and personalisation services, and content rows and analytics. The list also covers regional broadcast extras and streaming apps not used here.

It was applied in batches with a screenshot after each. Home, the inputs (HDMI 1, 2, 3 and Composite), Settings and the app grid all worked, and every change survived a full restart. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

What always stays on

  • The inputs, including HDMI 1 to 3 and Composite
  • Chromecast, which does not depend on the setup helper
  • Settings and the app grid

Good to know on this model

  • This model offers only Wireless debugging. After a restart, Android switches it off again, so turn it back on in Developer options.
  • The stock home screen is switched off last, after the new one is installed and checked.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Yes. Chromecast was checked and does not depend on the setup helper.

Yes. All the changes survived a full restart on this set.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
