Debloat the TCL 58S571F Fire TV

Fire OS 8 · 32 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This TCL Fire TV runs Fire OS 8 and ships with Shop on Fire TV, content recognition that sees what is on screen including HDMI, sponsored rows, autoplaying previews and an advertising ID. It also uploads Wi-Fi and performance metrics and device logs, and carries extras such as an operator app, Luna, News, Gallery and a system status monitor.

Fire TV keeps Amazon's home screen, so this is a clean-up only. The list was lived with for several reboots, and Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, Stremio, AirPlay from an iPhone and a Mac, and Settings all worked. TidyTV saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

Fire TV: TidyTV turns the apps off but keeps Amazon's home screen, because Fire OS won't let another home screen take over.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my TCL 58S571F?

Yes. Alexa voice stays on this list, and it turns itself back on at every boot.

Does casting still work?

Yes. AirPlay and Miracast are kept and were tested.

Will it stop the ads on the home screen?

It turns off sponsored and recommendation rows, autoplaying previews and the advertising ID. Amazon's home screen itself stays.
