Debloat the Philips 55OLED708

Google TV 12 · 30 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Philips OLED runs Google TV and ships with a Samba TV viewing tracker, TP Vision telemetry, Philips and Google promos, a retail demo and first-setup leftovers. The list also covers Philips features this owner does not use and preinstalled apps in the user app area.

It was applied in small batches, with a screenshot and a 30 second idle-log check after each. No retry loop appeared. Home, HDMI 2 with a PS5, Live TV, Settings and YouTube kept working. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

Google TV setup wizard 1

Its RecoveryActivity claims Home at priority 1 and takes over once the stock launcher is gone. The Wi-Fi network joined during first setup belonged to it: forget and re-join the network in Settings FIRST (it then belongs to com.android.tv.settings), or the TV drops off Wi-Fi. Networks it still owns are forgotten when it is disabled. Re-enable it before a factory reset.

What always stays on

  • Live TV (Philips PlayTV)
  • HDMI, component and composite inputs (the MediaTek TV input service)
  • AirPlay
  • Google Assistant
  • Firmware updates

Good to know on this model

  • Forget and re-join your Wi-Fi first. The setup wizard may own the saved network and Android deletes it when the wizard is switched off.
  • Casting this owner does not use is off, but AirPlay is kept.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Does the Samba TV tracker get turned off?

Yes. The Samba TV viewing tracker, which comes preinstalled, is on the list.

Will Google Assistant still work?

Yes. Google Assistant is kept on purpose, and so are AirPlay and firmware updates.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
