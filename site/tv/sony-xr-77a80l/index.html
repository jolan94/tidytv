Batch 4 1

Google TV setup wizard. Its RecoveryActivity claims HOME (priority 1) and would keep Home away from the new launcher once launcherx is disabled. Re-enable before a factory reset. Cost on this firmware: com.sony.dtv.sonysystemservice then looks up setupwraith's provider every 500 ms ("Failed to find provider info for com.google.android.tungsten.setupwraith", ~7,000 log lines an hour); it stopped the moment setupwraith was enabled again. Idle CPU still went from 22.6% to 9% overall. Ambient mode's "Select albums" needs both launcherx and setupwraith enabled: with launcherx off it closes at once (AccountModeContentProvider missing), with setupwraith off it says "Unable to load content". Casting with setupwraith off: mediashell logs "Failed to find provider info for ...CastInfoProvider" and "Got empty cast Id from setup, falling back to MediaShell", but no "No private key" or "Cannot get device credentials" line in a whole boot's log, and cast_shell kept logging "MdnsAppFilter: Done announcing."

What always stays on

Play Store, Play Services, keyboards, TV inputs, the remote and Bluetooth pairing, Settings and setup apps are always kept, whatever a list says.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Source: the sony-xr-77a80l list in cleanyourtv by Gathin (MIT), tested on this model. TidyTV re-checks every app against its own protected list before each change.
