Debloat the Xiaomi TV Box S (3rd gen)

Google TV 14 · 25 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The Xiaomi TV Box S (3rd gen) ships with Xiaomi analytics and a quality-stats uploader. It also has PatchWall content rows, promos and auto-installs, plus preloaded apps such as Disney+, HBO Max, YouTube Music, Play Games, a Mi video player and a gallery.

This list was tested for one evening on Google TV 14, covering the home screen, the app grid, the D-pad and a reboot. It has not been lived with for days yet. The voice assistant and the remote service stay on so the remote's mic works. TidyTV turns the apps off over Wi-Fi with a backup first and deletes nothing.

voice assistant, phone remote, Mi cast/share (the mic button stops working) KEPT ON here, measured on firmware V816.0.7.0.UZFAABX on two units: the mic button does NOT come back by re-enabling katniss alone. com.google.android.tv.remote.service, listed below, is the Google TV bridge for the physical remote: with it off, the remote's Bluetooth microphone never reaches the assistant, so the button opens the Assistant and it records silence. What always stays on

  • Google Assistant, so the remote's mic button works
  • The Google TV remote service, which the mic also needs
  • Xiaomi setup partner and TV services (mitv.service)
  • Amlogic services (com.droidlogic*)
  • Mi cast and share, phone remote, and the Play Store

Good to know on this model

  • The mic needs both the assistant and the remote service. Turning off only one of them leaves the button recording silence, so both are kept on.
  • The setup wizard takes over Home once the stock home is off, so it has to be turned off first. Re-enable it to pair a new remote.
  • The Wi-Fi network saved during first setup belongs to the setup wizard. Join it again by hand in Settings first, or turning the wizard off can drop Wi-Fi.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my Xiaomi box?

Yes. The voice assistant and the remote service both stay on, because the remote's mic needs both of them.

Will it remove the PatchWall rows and promos?

Yes. PatchWall, its content rows, promos and the auto-install setup are turned off. The apps are only switched off, not uninstalled.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
