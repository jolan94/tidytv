Debloat the PRISM+ Q65 Ultra QLED

Android TV 14 · 25 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The PRISM+ Q65 Ultra is a Realtek-based Google TV set made by MOKA. It ships with tuner apps for DTMB, ISDB and ATSC broadcast TV that this set does not use, two karaoke apps, a Max app, an ambient screensaver, and a service that is likely screen mirroring. It also has a Wi-Fi hotspot app, a meeting service and a sticker app.

This list was used on one set for a few days. On the test day, the HDMI input, ARC audio to a soundbar and Home all worked. Nothing else was checked, so test the things you use after the change. TidyTV turns these apps off from your phone over the same Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and deletes nothing.

What always stays on

  • Live TV and the other inputs: the Realtek TV and ATV services stay on, because HDMI and the other inputs need them.
  • Play Store, Play services, keyboards, the remote and Bluetooth pairing are always kept by TidyTV.
  • Settings and setup apps are always kept too.
  • The stock Google TV home is turned off last, after a new home screen is running.

Good to know on this model

  • Turning off the stock home before a new home screen is running can land Home on a factory test app.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will HDMI still work on my PRISM+ Q65 Ultra?

On the test set, yes. The HDMI input, ARC audio to a soundbar and Home all worked after the list was applied. Other inputs were not checked.

Does casting still work?

The screen mirroring service is turned off. Its function was only likely screen mirroring, so check casting from your phone after the change.

Will it stop the ads on the home screen?

The list does not name any home screen ads. It turns off the Max app, the ambient screensaver and a few preloaded services, so the home screen may still show what it did before.
