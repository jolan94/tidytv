Debloat the Hisense 55QD6QF Fire TV

Fire OS 8.1.8.0 · 52 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This list was tested on a real Hisense 55QD6QF Fire TV. TidyTV uses it to turn off 52 apps over Wi-Fi from your phone, after saving a backup. Nothing is deleted, and one tap puts it all back.

Fire TV: TidyTV turns the apps off but keeps Amazon's home screen, because Fire OS won't let another home screen take over.

What always stays on

Play Store, Play Services, keyboards, TV inputs, the remote and Bluetooth pairing, Settings and setup apps are always kept, whatever a list says.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Source: the hisense-fire-tv-55qd6qf list in cleanyourtv by Gathin (MIT), tested on this model. TidyTV re-checks every app against its own protected list before each change.
