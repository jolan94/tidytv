Debloat the TCL 43S450F-CA Fire TV

Fire OS 8 · 45 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This TCL S4 Fire TV Edition ships with Amazon metrics and usage reporting, sponsored Sneak Peek previews, the advertising ID, recommendation rows, upgrade promos and Shop on Fire TV. It also reports what is on screen and uploads Wi-Fi passwords to Amazon. Preloaded apps such as Silk, Amazon Photos and Kids add to the load.

Fire TV keeps Amazon's home screen, so this is a clean-up only, with no replacement launcher. TidyTV turns the listed apps off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything. Each app can be switched back on in the app.

Fire TV: TidyTV turns the apps off but keeps Amazon's home screen, because Fire OS won't let another home screen take over.

Good to know on this model

  • Amazon signs the input-switching service, so HDMI switching stays with Amazon's own apps.
  • Several preloads are off by the owner's choice, such as Silk, Crave and Paramount+. Switch back on any you use.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my TCL 43S450F-CA?

Yes. The owner kept Alexa and its voice services.

Will it stop the ads on the home screen?

It turns off the sponsored rows, Sneak Peek previews, upgrade promos, the advertising ID and Shop on Fire TV. Amazon's home screen itself stays.

Does casting still work?

Yes. AirPlay, Miracast and Amazon's casting services are kept.
