Google TV setup wizard 1

Its RecoveryActivity claims HOME and beats the new launcher once com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx is disabled; `pm disable` of that one component is refused, so the whole package has to go for a launcher swap. It also owns saved Wi-Fi networks, so check first: `dumpsys wifi | grep -o 'cname=[^ ]*'`. If the network the TV is actually connected to belongs to setupwraith, forget and re-join it in Settings before running this, or the disable deletes that network and takes the TV off Wi-Fi and ADB. Needs FORCE_SETUPWRAITH=1 to pass disable.sh's Wi-Fi guard.

Good to know on this model

  • Three Hisense services must never be turned off. Turning them off sent a sister model to Android Recovery.
  • The streaming apps and voice group is your choice. Keep the ones you use.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Will the voice button still work on my Hisense TV?

Yes. Google Assistant is kept by choice.

Does casting still work?

Yes. Chromecast, AirPlay, Miracast and DLNA are all kept.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns the apps back on and puts the Google TV home screen back in one tap.
