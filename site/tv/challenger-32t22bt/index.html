Debloat the Challenger 32T22 BT

Android 7 · 21 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Challenger 32-inch budget TV has only 1 GB of memory and 8 GB of storage. It ships with factory test and diagnostic tools, cleaner apps, third-party stores, background services, duplicate media apps and extra system apps and browsers. The list has 21 apps.

Turning these apps off freed memory for a new home screen and for streaming apps on the one TV it was tested on. Live TV, HDMI and the tuner keep working. How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will HDMI and live TV still work on my Challenger TV?

Yes. The TV service and the tuner agent that they need are kept.

Will it help a slow TV?

It was tested on this TV and freed memory for a new home screen and for streaming apps. The header gives no speed numbers.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns the apps back on and puts the vendor home screen back in one tap.
