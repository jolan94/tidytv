Debloat the Nokia Streaming Box 8010

Android TV 14 · 22 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Nokia box ships with ad tech, telemetry and feedback services, content rows and partner auto-installs, plus leftover factory and test apps. It also carries a preinstalled app called Hoppr that runs from start-up and asks for access to the microphone, contacts and location.

Unlike the Nokia 8000, this model accepts the normal switch-off. The list was checked after two restarts: every app stayed off, and Home and all your apps still worked. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

What always stays on

  • The Google Play Store and Play Services
  • Your streaming apps, which play as normal
  • Any app you leave switched on, since each has its own switch

Good to know on this model

  • Network debugging stays on after a restart here, so switch it off when you are done.
  • This is the 8010. The Nokia Streaming Box 8000 behaves differently and has its own list.
  • The stock home screen is replaced by a new one, so pick a clean home screen in the app first.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

What is Hoppr and does TidyTV turn it off?

It is a preinstalled app that runs from start-up and asks for the microphone, contacts and location. Yes, the list turns it off.

Will my streaming apps still play?

Yes. In testing, all apps played after the list was applied and after two restarts.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
