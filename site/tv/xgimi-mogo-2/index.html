Debloat the XGIMI MoGo 2

Android TV 11 · 8 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This XGIMI projector ships with telemetry, bug reports and feedback tools and a few preloaded apps. The list is short, 8 apps, and covers only what was found on this firmware. Each app has its own switch in the app, so you decide which ones to turn off.

It was applied on a real unit and rebooted twice, then used for about a day. The projector's own hardware services were all left on: auto keystone, ToF autofocus, the HDMI input player, the Bluetooth remote and key handling. TidyTV turns these apps off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

Good to know on this model

  • Both USB debugging and Network debugging must be on, or the connection stays closed.
  • The Play Store says the Netflix TV app is not compatible. Netflix's regular Android app is a workaround.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Can I get the old home screen back on my XGIMI MoGo 2?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap. TidyTV only switches apps off, so nothing is ever deleted.

Will the remote's mic button still work?

Yes. Voice is not switched off here, and the mic button worked in testing.

Does TidyTV delete anything on my XGIMI MoGo 2?

No. It turns apps off with Android's own setting, saves a backup first and checks each one. You can turn any app back on with its switch.
