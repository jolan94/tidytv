Debloat the Philips Smart 4K TV

Google TV 14 · 17 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Philips is built by Skyworth and ships with Skyworth recommendation rows, an after-sale tracker that runs at boot and watches network changes and location, a background management service, and a My Apps screen. It also has an on-screen manual, a casting service that bundles analytics, and preloaded Google extras.

This list was tested on the day it was applied, so it has not had days of use yet. The Live TV app (CanaliTV) stays on, and so does the speech recognition service, Google text-to-speech. TidyTV turns these items off from your phone over Wi-Fi. It saves a backup first, switches each app off without deleting it, and stops if anything looks wrong.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my Philips Smart 4K TV?

Not while Google Assistant is off. This list turns it off by choice, and the microphone button does nothing until you turn it back on.

Does casting still work?

Casting is off by choice in this list. The Chromecast, DIAL, and Skyworth AirPlay and Miracast services are the ones to keep if you cast from a phone or mirror a screen.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
