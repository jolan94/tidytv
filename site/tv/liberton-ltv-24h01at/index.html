Debloat the Liberton LTV 24H01AT

Android TV 11 · 20 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Liberton set is built by Changhong and runs Android TV 11 with the Homwee layer on top. It ships with a Homwee app store, silent installers, ad services, sponsored rows on the home screen and extras like an accessibility service and preloaded apps. Apps such as Kyivstar TV and Spotify come preinstalled.

The list was checked against the 122 packages on this TV. The owner chose to keep the voice assistant and Chromecast casting. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything, so each app can be switched back on.

What always stays on

  • Voice assistant
  • Chromecast casting
  • Remote key service and the Input key
  • Live TV, its channel scan and all the MediaTek TV parts
  • A Google home screen part that Live TV needs: without it the Input key and other hotkeys stop working after a restart
  • Factory tools (never opened)

Good to know on this model

  • The vendor updaters are left on the list but commented out here, so updates keep working unless you turn them off.
  • Do not switch off the Google partner customizer. Without it the TV service crashes after a restart and the Input key stops working.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the Input key still work?

Yes, as long as you leave the parts listed as kept alone. The Google partner customizer matters most: the Input key breaks without it.

Does casting still work?

Yes. Chromecast is kept by the owner.

Does it have a way back?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on in one tap, and puts the old home screen back if you changed it.
