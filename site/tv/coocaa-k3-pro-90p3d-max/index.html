Debloat the Coocaa K3 Pro 90P3D Max

Coocaa OS (Android 11) · 23 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Skyworth Coocaa K3 Pro runs the China-market Coocaa OS on Android 11. It ships with vendor content and preinstalled apps. The list has 23 apps in two groups and is the first China-market Coocaa list here. It shares nothing with Skyworth's Google TV sets.

The Coocaa home screen is never turned off, because the TV hangs on its logo and needs a factory reset if it is. TidyTV leaves it on. It turns the rest off from your phone with a backup first and deletes nothing. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

Good to know on this model

  • Never turn off the Coocaa home screen. Doing so hung the TV on the logo and the remote stopped working, and only a factory reset fixed it.
  • This firmware hides Developer options. Debugging comes from a secret factory menu and switches off again at every restart.
  • Almost no app shows on the home grid, so the apps need adding by hand.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

What happens if the Coocaa home screen is turned off?

The TV hangs on the Coocaa logo and the remote stops working, and only a factory reset fixes it. TidyTV keeps it on.

Why does debugging switch itself off?

This firmware turns it off at every restart. You switch it on again from the factory menu.

Can I get everything back if I change my mind?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app TidyTV turned off back on in one tap.
