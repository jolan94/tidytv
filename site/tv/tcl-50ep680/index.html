Debloat the TCL 50EP680

Android TV 11 · 47 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The TCL 50EP680 ships with TCL analytics and promo services, including one that pushes TCL content to the home screen at boot. It also has Google's recommendation rows, Play Movies, a screensaver, a set of preloaded games, news and remote support apps. Three voice assistant apps are in the list too, grouped as off by choice.

The list was applied on a real unit, and every app was then checked one by one as off. Home, the app grid, the Inputs menu with HDMI 1 to 3, AV and Live TV, the Wi-Fi pill and Settings all still worked afterwards. The TV was not restarted, and the list has not been used for days yet. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, with a backup first.

What always stays on

  • Live TV, HDMI and the sources menu, through TCL's Live TV app and its input service.
  • Casting: Chromecast, Miracast and TCL's multi-screen app.
  • Streaming apps such as Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, HBO Max and Spotify.
  • Firmware updates, Bluetooth remote pairing and the Play Store.

Good to know on this model

  • The UK catch-up apps are already off from the factory on this set, so the list does not include them.
  • Three apps came back on overnight on the same TCL board: the copy-data helper, the TCL dashboard and the partner installer. Check them again after a day or two.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my TCL 50EP680?

Only if the voice apps stay on. Google Assistant, Google's TV assistant and TCL's assistant are off by choice in the list, so leave their switches on if you use the mic button.

Does casting still work?

Yes. Chromecast, Miracast and TCL's multi-screen app are kept on purpose, so casting from a phone keeps working.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
