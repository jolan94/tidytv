Debloat the Hisense E5Q Pro

VIDAA on Android 11 · 18 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This China-market Hisense E5Q Pro runs VIDAA on Android 11. It ships with Hisense advertising, telemetry, push messages, store, hotel and demo modes, preloaded content and gimmicks, a first-run setup and a screensaver of ambient photos. The list has 18 apps.

It was applied and used for a few days with no crashes and a normal boot. The inputs, HDMI-CEC and the Sogou keyboard kept working. The VIDAA home screen is left alone. TidyTV turns the rest off over Wi-Fi with a backup first and deletes nothing. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the VIDAA home screen change?

No. This list keeps the stock home screen and only removes the ad, telemetry, push and preloaded layer on top of it.

Will firmware updates still arrive?

Yes. The update services are kept on purpose.

Do HDMI and HDMI-CEC still work?

Yes. They kept working after a few days of use.
