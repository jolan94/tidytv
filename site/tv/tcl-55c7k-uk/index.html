Debloat the TCL 55C7K

Google TV 14 · 32 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This UK TCL QD-Mini LED runs Google TV on Android 14. It ships with Samba TV, which tracks what you watch, and TCL analytics, log upload and account sign-in nags. It also carries TCL Live FAST channels, TCL content pushed to the home screen at boot, push messages, retail demo apps and a cleaner.

The list was applied on a real unit, though not yet lived with for days. Wi-Fi stayed up, the Inputs menu listed HDMI 1 to 4 and Live TV, and after a reboot Home and Wi-Fi came back. TidyTV saves a backup first, never deletes anything and lets you switch any app back on.

Good to know on this model

  • Wi-Fi saved during first setup may belong to the setup app. Forget and rejoin it in Settings before that app is turned off.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my TCL 55C7K?

Not with the default list, which turns off Google Assistant voice by the owner's choice. Switch it back on in the app if you use the mic button.

Will it stop the ads on the home screen?

It turns off the TCL content rows, Live FAST channels, TCL tweaks to the Google TV home and the push messages and promos.

Does casting still work?

Miracast and TCL's T-Cast phone mirroring are on the list by choice and switched off. Switch them back on in the app if you use them.
