Debloat the Magcubic HY300 Pro+ projector

Android 14 · 2 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This list was tested on a real Magcubic HY300 Pro+ projector. TidyTV uses it to turn off 2 apps over Wi-Fi from your phone, after saving a backup. Nothing is deleted, and one tap puts it all back.

Apps TidyTV can turn off on the Magcubic HY300 Pro+ projector

Grouped as the tested list groups them. In the app each one has a switch, so you choose. Apps marked your call are extras the list leaves on.

What always stays on

  • com.htc.magcubicos: Home, disabled by the install script, not by this list.
  • com.softwinner.awsource, com.softwinner.vis, com.softwinner.awlivetv: the inputs.
  • com.hysd.vafocus: auto keystone and autofocus.
  • com.hy.hyotaupdate, com.softwinner.update: firmware updaters.
  • com.android.ytb, com.android.nfx: accessibility services behind the remote's YouTube and Netflix keys.
  • Factory and test packages (com.htc.hyk_test, com.htc.hyk_pcba, com.softwinner.tv.factorymenu).
  • com.toofifi.lineserver and com.mphotool.usbcastserver ship disabled.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Source: the magcubic-hy300-pro-plus list in cleanyourtv by Gathin (MIT), tested on this model. TidyTV re-checks every app against its own protected list before each change.
