Debloat the Magcubic HY300 Pro

Android 11 · 14 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Magcubic projector runs Android 11 on HTC and Allwinner firmware. It ships with nine apps nobody asked for, three printing services, and screensaver and backup extras. The list is short and mild, because the projector's own tools are needed for day-to-day use.

The projector's own settings (Wi-Fi, picture, keystone), the firmware updaters and the HDMI and AV input services are left alone. TidyTV turns the rest off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything. Each app has its own switch.

What always stays on

  • The projector's own settings: Wi-Fi, picture and keystone
  • The firmware updaters
  • Factory and test tools (never opened)
  • HDMI 1 to 3 and AV input services

Good to know on this model

  • The original home screen stays enabled. A new home screen can still take over as Home.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will keystone and picture settings still work?

Yes. The projector's own settings app is kept.

Will the HDMI inputs still work?

Yes. The input and source services are kept, and HDMI 1 to 3 and AV stay in the Inputs menu.

Does it have a way back?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on in one tap, and puts the old home screen back if you changed it.
