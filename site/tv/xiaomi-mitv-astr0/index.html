Debloat the Xiaomi Redmi Smart TV A Pro 55 (2025)

Android 14 (China) · 26 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Redmi Smart TV from China ships with Xiaomi telemetry, ads and push, preloaded apps, Mi Home and a cleaner. Its standby screensaver also shows a carousel of ads downloaded from the internet. Each app has its own switch in the app, so you decide which ones to turn off.

It was disabled and lived with on 22 October, and the screensaver-ad app was seen running while an ad showed. TidyTV saves a backup first and never deletes anything. The stock launcher stays on, since it was replaced by a special setup. TidyTV turns these apps off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Can I get the old home screen back on my Xiaomi Redmi Smart TV A Pro 55?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap. TidyTV only switches apps off, so nothing is ever deleted.

Will it stop the screensaver ads?

Yes. The app that shows them is on the list and was checked while an ad was showing.

Does TidyTV delete anything on my Xiaomi Redmi Smart TV A Pro 55?

No. It turns apps off with Android's own setting, saves a backup first and checks each one. You can turn any app back on with its switch.
