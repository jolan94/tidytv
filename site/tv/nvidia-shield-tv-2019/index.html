Debloat the NVIDIA SHIELD TV (2019 tube)

Android TV 11 · 33 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The SHIELD TV tube ships with NVIDIA usage statistics, crash and feedback reporters, a registration service and a first-run promo. It also carries partner auto-installs, a beta programme, the NVIDIA Games app, a legacy photo viewer, file-sharing services and a Google data-saver VPN. Together they add up to a lot of background apps you never asked for.

This list was tested on SHIELD Experience 9.2.4 with Android 11. The SHIELD TV phone remote app, Chromecast built-in, Google Assistant and Prime Video all kept working. TidyTV turns the apps off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first, and deletes nothing, so you can undo it in one tap.

Good to know on this model

  • The sponsored rows and Top picks come from the recommendations service. It stays on because turning it off breaks the SHIELD TV phone app's remote keys, so this list does not remove those rows from the stock home screen.
  • Installing a clean home screen replaces the stock one, so those rows are not what you see day to day.
  • A SHIELD update can turn apps back on. Run Check my TV afterwards.
  • On the tested unit, firmware updates were turned off by choice. Turn them back on to get NVIDIA security patches or before contacting NVIDIA support.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the SHIELD TV phone remote app still work?

Yes. The list keeps the recommendations service on because the phone app's keys stop working without it. The remote app itself also stays on.

Will this stop the sponsored rows on the home screen?

Not on the stock home screen, because the service that feeds them has to stay on. If you switch to a clean home screen, it replaces the stock one, so you do not see those rows.

Does casting still work, and can I undo everything?

Chromecast built-in stays on. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on and brings back the old home screen in one tap.
