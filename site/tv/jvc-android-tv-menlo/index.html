Debloat the JVC Android TV (Vestel)

Android TV 11 · 25 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This JVC Android TV, built by Vestel for Spain, ships with Vestel telemetry and a cleaner app. It also has Google and partner attribution, feedback and auto-installs, preloaded apps and background services nobody needs on a TV. The list has 25 apps. It uses the classic Android TV home.

It was disabled in two batches on one TV. Prime Video and Disney+ were kept at the owner's choice. The Input key, Live TV, Chromecast built-in and Gboard keep working. TidyTV turns the rest off from your phone with a backup first and deletes nothing. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

What always stays on

  • The Input key handler, which looks like retail demo mode
  • Live TV and the TV service
  • Chromecast built-in, Play Store and Play Services
  • Gboard and text to speech
  • The Vestel TV, key, backlight and remote update services
  • Remote pairing and Dolby audio

Good to know on this model

  • One MediaTek home screen helper crashes the TV service if it is turned off, so it stays on.
  • There is no separate firmware update app on this TV. Updates live in Settings.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the Input button still work on my JVC TV?

Yes. The app that handles it looks like bloat, but it must stay on, so TidyTV keeps it.

Does casting still work?

Yes. Chromecast built-in is kept.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns the apps back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
