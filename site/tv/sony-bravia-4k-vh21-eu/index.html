Debloat the Sony BRAVIA XR-55A80K

Google TV 12 · 26 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This 2022 Sony OLED runs Google TV on Android 12. It ships with a viewing tracker that identifies what is on screen, telemetry and remote support, Sony ads and recommendations, and a demo or shop mode. The list also covers a voice assistant and apps off by choice.

The Sony parts that drive HDMI, the tuner, the Input overlay, Bravia Sync, updates and the remote's app buttons are kept. Many UK broadcaster launchers come switched off from the factory on this set. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

What always stays on

  • Netflix, YouTube and Apple TV
  • Chromecast built in and AirPlay and HomeKit
  • Firmware updates
  • Sony IP control, which Home Assistant needs
  • The HDMI, tuner and Bravia Sync services
  • The remote's app button services

Good to know on this model

  • The voice assistant is off by choice here. The remote's mic button stops working.
  • The remote's Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video and Disney+ buttons keep working after the stock home screen is off. YouTube, Prime Video and Disney+ share one key code, so remapping one needs care.
  • The remote's Settings button does nothing once the stock home screen is off, until a helper service is on.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my Sony BRAVIA?

Not with the default list, because the voice assistant is off by choice. Switch it back on in the app if you use voice.

Will Home Assistant still see my TV?

Yes. Sony IP control is kept, and without it Home Assistant shows the TV as off.

Will it stop the viewing tracker?

Yes. The ACR viewing tracker is the first group on the list.
