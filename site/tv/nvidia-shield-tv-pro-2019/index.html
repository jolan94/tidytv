Debloat the NVIDIA SHIELD TV Pro (2019)

Android TV 11 · 10 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The SHIELD TV Pro ships with NVIDIA usage statistics, crash and feedback reporters and a log collector. It also has partner auto-installs, the NVIDIA Games app, the old GameStream receiver (GameStream closed in 2023), a legacy photo viewer and a print service most people never use.

This list was tested on SHIELD Experience 9.2.4 with Android 11, and the package names were also checked on 9.2.2. The remote, controllers and Bluetooth, audio and display services, and the SHIELD TV phone app all stay on. TidyTV turns the apps off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first, and deletes nothing.

Disabled, Android stops binding the SHIELD TV phone app's input service (com.nvidia.shield.remote.server, virtualinput.InputService, a TvRemoteProvider) at boot: the app still connects, but every key fails ("Key injection failed", "Beyonder_VIS ... isInitialized=false"). Bisected with a reboot per step on a 2017 SHIELD (darcy) and confirmed on a 2015/2017 one (foster_e), SHIELD Experience on Android 11 (docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md). What always stays on

  • The recommendations service (com.google.android.tvrecommendations), needed by the SHIELD TV phone app's remote keys
  • The remote, game controllers and Bluetooth pairing
  • AI upscaling, display and audio services
  • Watch Next and Live Channels data
  • TV settings
  • Google Play Services and the NVIDIA key-combo update service

Good to know on this model

  • The sponsored rows and Top picks come from the recommendations service. It stays on because turning it off breaks the SHIELD TV phone app's remote keys, so this list does not remove those rows from the stock home screen.
  • A clean home screen replaces the stock one, so those rows are not what you see day to day.
  • A SHIELD update can turn apps back on. Run Check my TV afterwards.
  • Netflix on the remote is handled by the firmware, so turning Netflix off does not free that button.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the SHIELD TV phone remote app still work?

Yes. The recommendations service stays on because the phone app's keys fail without it, and the remote and Bluetooth services are left alone.

Will it stop the sponsored rows on the home screen?

Not on the stock home screen, because the service that feeds them must stay on. A clean home screen replaces the stock one, so you would not see them there.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap. Nothing is uninstalled.
