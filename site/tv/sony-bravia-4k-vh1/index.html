Debloat the Sony BRAVIA 4K VH1

Google TV 12 · 38 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Sony BRAVIA VH1, sold as the KD-55XH9096 in Europe, runs Google TV 12. It ships with a viewing tracker, telemetry and remote support, demo, shop and hotel modes, Sony promos and Select, and Google rows and preloads. The list has 38 apps, and three are off by the owner's choice.

It was lived with for weeks on a set in Spain, and Live TV, HDMI inputs, Ethernet and Chromecast kept working. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything. Each app has its own switch.

Good to know on this model

  • The Google TV setup wizard may take Home back after a restart. If that happens, switch it off too.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Does Chromecast still work?

Yes. Chromecast is kept.

Will firmware updates still arrive?

Yes. The Sony update services are kept.

Does it have a way back?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on in one tap, and puts the old home screen back if you changed it.
