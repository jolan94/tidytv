Debloat the Philips 75PUS8505

Android TV 12 · 19 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This 75 inch Philips set from 2020 runs Android TV 12 and ships with TP Vision telemetry, Philips content promos and an app gallery, a retail demo and Google first-setup leftovers. The list also covers voice assistants and Play-Fi speaker apps this owner does not use.

Every entry was checked on this TV before it went on the list. The set has little memory, and its home screen can be replaced by a lighter one. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

DTS Play-Fi, wireless multi-room speakers 1

It holds MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS and may capture audio playback, but nothing in the system binds it and dumpsys audio was identical before and after: output stayed on HDMI ARC (mMainType=0x8) to a soundbar, and sound and remote volume were checked by ear afterwards. Keep both if you own Play-Fi speakers. The app itself does not stay disabled: after the next reboot it was back at enabled=1, with "AudioOutService: PlayFi Enabled" logged during boot, as examples/philips-tpm191e and examples/philips-55oled707 also found. So it is commented out; the soft AP stayed disabled.

What always stays on

  • Live TV (Philips PlayTV)
  • The Sources drawer service, which breaks if it is switched off
  • The DLNA media player, which the Media app uses
  • Firmware updates

Good to know on this model

  • The very first start of a new home screen can be slow on this set. Give it a minute before trying again.
  • The Sources drawer stops working if its service is switched off. TidyTV keeps it on.
  • Netflix, Prime Video and Freeview apps are already off from the factory on a Czech unit.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the Sources button still work?

Yes. The service behind the Sources drawer is kept, because the drawer stops working without it.

Do I need to keep Play-Fi apps?

The list switches off one Play-Fi part, the speaker setup helper. If you use DTS Play-Fi wireless speakers, switch it back on in the app.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
