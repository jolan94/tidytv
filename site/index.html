More than a clean-up.

Once your TV is connected, TidyTV is handy for more than tidying.

Your phone is the remote

Use the D-pad, volume, power and playback keys, type on the TV, or open any TV app from a list. It works on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV.

Send files to the TV

Pick photos, videos, music or documents on your phone. They land in Downloads › TidyTV on the TV, and nothing is ever overwritten.

My TV

One hub with Tidy up, Remote, Send files, Check my TV, Home screen and Restore. Pick the Simple or Apple TV style home screen, or Projectivy from your TV's Play Store. Give the Apple TV style a look: Aurora, Glass, Scenes, or a photo from your phone. Saved TVs reconnect in one tap.

Check my TV

TV updates sometimes turn apps back on. Check my TV lists them, and one tap turns them off again.

Three quick steps.

1

Turn on developer mode

On the TV: Settings, Device Preferences, About, press Build seven times. Then switch on Wireless debugging (some TVs call it Network debugging). On Ethernet, turn on the TV's Wi-Fi first, or it stays greyed out.

    Questions, answered

    Does TidyTV delete apps?

    No. Apps are turned off with Android's own setting, never uninstalled, and Restore turns them back on.

    Why is my Google TV so slow, and will this help?

    Many TVs ship with apps for ads, tracking and suggestions that run in the background and use memory. TidyTV turns those off and shows how much memory was freed. How much faster it feels depends on the TV.

    How do I debloat an Android TV without a PC?

    Install TidyTV on an Android phone on the same Wi-Fi, turn on developer options and wireless debugging on the TV, then tap your TV in the app. It connects over Wi-Fi with ADB, so no computer or cable is needed.

    Do I need a computer?

    No. Your phone and TV only need to be on the same Wi-Fi. No laptop, no AI, no root.

    My TV update turned apps back on. What now?

    Open My TV and tap Check my TV. It lists apps that came back and new ones on your TV's list. Tap Turn these off again, and TidyTV saves a fresh backup first. Restart the TV and check reboots the TV and checks again.

    Can I undo changes?

    Yes. Restore everything puts every app and your old home screen back with one tap.

    Does it work with my TV?

    Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes. Fire TV gets the clean-up but keeps Amazon's home screen. Samsung and LG don't run Android.

    Where do I get TidyTV?

    From Google Play on your Android phone. For phones without Google Play, every release is also on GitHub as a signed APK.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Free and open source under the MIT licence. No ads, no account, no tracking. The app only asks GitHub once a day whether a newer version is out.

    Will it remove YouTube ads?

    No. It turns off the TV maker's ads and trackers. Ads inside apps are not affected.

    Can I use my phone as the TV remote?

    Yes. After you connect, open My TV and tap Remote. It works over the same network connection, so no extra pairing is needed, and it works on Fire TV too.

    Where do files I send end up?

    In the Downloads folder on the TV, in a folder called TidyTV. Open the TV's file manager or media player to find them.
